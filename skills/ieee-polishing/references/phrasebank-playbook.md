# IEEE Technical Phrasebank Playbook

## Stating contributions
- "This paper presents a novel approach to..."
- "We propose [method], which addresses [problem] by..."
- "The key contribution of this work is..."

## Describing results
- "[Method] achieves a [X]% improvement over [baseline] on [metric]"
- "As shown in Table [n], [method] outperforms..."
- "The results demonstrate that..."

## Comparing with prior work
- "Unlike [n], which requires [limitation], our method..."
- "While [n] reports [result], we observe [result] under [condition]"
- "This is consistent with [n] but extends their finding by..."

## Acknowledging limitations
- "A limitation of this study is..."
- "We note that these results are based on [dataset/condition]"
- "Future work should investigate..."
