# IEEE Writing Strategy Overview

## The IEEE writing mindset
IEEE writing is technical communication, not literary prose. The goal is to transfer understanding from author to reader with minimum friction.

## Core strategy
1. **Claim first, then evidence** — Every section starts with the point, then supports it
2. **Numbers over adjectives** — "15% improvement" beats "significant improvement"
3. **One idea per paragraph** — If you have two ideas, you need two paragraphs
4. **Explicit over implicit** — "Because X, we observe Y" not "X leads to Y"
5. **Cite everything factual** — Claims without [n] look like opinion

## The 3-pass review
1. **Structural**: Does the argument flow? Do sections do their job?
2. **Evidential**: Are claims supported? Are citations present?
3. **Stylistic**: Sentences ≤ 35 words? Active voice? No filler words?
