# IEEE Introduction Polishing

## Job: Field → bottleneck → gap → present study

## Structural moves
1. **Field opening** — Broad significance (1-2 sentences)
2. **Bottleneck** — What limits current approaches (2-3 sentences)
3. **Prior attempts** — What others have tried and their limitations (3-4 sentences with citations)
4. **Unresolved gap** — What remains unsolved (1-2 sentences)
5. **Present study** — What this paper does and its contributions (2-3 sentences)
6. **Paper organization** — Roadmap paragraph (optional, 2-3 sentences)
