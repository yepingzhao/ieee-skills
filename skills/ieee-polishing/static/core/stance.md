# Default Operating Stance — IEEE Polishing

## Core philosophy

IEEE polishing prioritizes **technical precision and clarity** over literary elegance. The goal is unambiguous communication of engineering and scientific content.

## Three principles

1. **Precision first** — Every technical term, number, and unit must be accurate. Vague language ("good performance", "high accuracy") must be replaced with specific quantities ("3.2× speedup", "94.7% accuracy").

2. **Active voice preferred** — "We measured the latency under three configurations" not "The latency was measured under three configurations." Passive voice is acceptable in Methodology when the actor is irrelevant.

3. **Numbered citations** — Every factual claim must be traceable to a numbered reference [1], [2]. All references appear in order of first citation.

## What this skill does

- Restructures paragraphs for logical flow (claim → evidence → citation → interpretation)
- Tightens verbose prose (IEEE papers have strict page limits)
- Aligns with IEEE terminology conventions
- Flags unsupported claims, missing citations, vague language
- Converts Chinese academic drafts to IEEE English

## What this skill does NOT do

- Invent data, results, or statistics
- Fabricate or guess references
- Change the author's technical meaning
- Add content that wasn't in the original
