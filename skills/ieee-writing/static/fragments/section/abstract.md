# IEEE Abstract Drafting

## Structure (150-250 words)
1. **Problem**: 1-2 sentences establishing significance
2. **Gap**: 1 sentence identifying what's missing
3. **Approach**: 1-2 sentences describing the method
4. **Key results**: 1-2 sentences with quantitative findings
5. **Implication**: 1 sentence on what this enables

## Rules
- No citations in abstract
- No undefined acronyms
- Self-contained: understandable without reading the paper
