# IEEE Review Standards

## Source material
This skill is built from IEEE publication guidelines, reviewer forms, and published peer-review criteria for IEEE Transactions and conferences.

## IEEE review criteria
1. **Novelty and Contribution**: Does the paper advance the state of the art?
2. **Technical Correctness**: Are the methods, analysis, and conclusions valid?
3. **Experimental Validation**: Are claims supported by adequate experiments?
4. **Presentation Quality**: Is the paper clear, well-organized, and free of errors?
5. **Relevance**: Is the topic appropriate for the venue?
