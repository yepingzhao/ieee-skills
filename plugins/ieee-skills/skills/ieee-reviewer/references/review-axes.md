# IEEE Review Scoring Axes

| Axis | Weight | Key questions |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Novelty | 30% | Is this new? How does it advance the field? |
| Soundness | 30% | Are methods correct? Are conclusions valid? |
| Rigor | 20% | Are experiments thorough? Ablations? Stats? |
| Clarity | 10% | Is the paper well-written? Figures clear? |
| Relevance | 10% | Appropriate for this venue? |
