# IEEE Presentation Contract

## Slide spine = scientific argument, not manuscript sections
1. Title slide (paper title, authors, venue)
2. Problem & Motivation (1-2 slides)
3. Key Insight / Approach (1-2 slides)
4. Method Overview (1-2 slides)
5. Key Results (2-4 slides, one per major finding)
6. Comparison to Prior Work (1 slide)
7. Limitations & Future Work (1 slide)
8. Summary / Takeaway (1 slide)
9. Thank you / Q&A (1 slide)

## Rules
- 10-15 slides for 15-min talk; 20-25 for 30-min
- One idea per slide
- Figures as evidence, not decoration
- No walls of text: bullets, not paragraphs
