# IEEE Introduction Drafting

## Structure
1. **Field context** (1-2 sentences): Broad significance
2. **Bottleneck** (2-3 sentences): What limits current approaches
3. **Prior attempts** (3-4 sentences with [n] citations): What's been tried, why insufficient
4. **Gap** (1-2 sentences): What remains unsolved
5. **Present work** (2-3 sentences): This paper's approach and contributions
6. **Organization** (optional): "The remainder of this paper is organized as follows..."
